How the relationship works.

Deliberately unglamorous. Most problems we are brought in to fix were caused by nobody owning the account.

One partner, named on the engagement letter

The person in your first meeting is the person who signs your accounts. No handover to a junior once the work starts.

Fixed fees, agreed before we begin

Quoted annually and billed monthly. If the scope changes we agree the new fee before any work happens, not on the invoice.

Replies within one working day

Including to say we need longer. Silence is the thing clients complain about most about their previous firm.

Questions people ask before signing.

What does it cost?

Annual accounts and tax for a company turning over under R10m typically run R28,000 to R45,000 a year. We quote exactly after a first meeting, and the quote does not move unless the scope does.

How do we move from our current accountant?

We write to them for professional clearance and your records. You sign one letter. It usually takes two to three weeks and there is no gap in your filings.

Do you work with businesses smaller than R2m?

Honestly, we are not the best value below that. We will point you to two firms who are set up for it.

Which accounting software do you work with?

Xero and Sage, both fine. If you are on spreadsheets we will migrate you as part of the first year and quote that separately.
